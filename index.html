Fart Raft Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Developer: RedGrapesGames

This policy explains what information the game Fart Raft (the "game"), made by RedGrapesGames ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to redgrapesgames01@gmail.com.

In short
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number.
  • You pick a nickname. The game adds a short tag to it (for example RAFTER #K7P2), and it is shown publicly on the leaderboards with your race times.
  • Voice chat is optional. It only works in multiplayer games after you allow the microphone and turn it on. It is sent live to the other players and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

Nickname and tag

When you first open the game you choose a nickname (up to 12 letters and numbers). Our server gives it a 4-character tag so that players with the same nickname can be told apart. Your nickname and tag are stored on our server and are public: anyone who plays the game can see them on the leaderboards, and the players you play with see your nickname. Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your nickname.

Device code

The game creates a random code on your device the first time it registers your nickname. It proves that the nickname is yours when you change it or send a score. We store only a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of this code, and it is not linked to your name, your phone number or your Google account.

Game results and progress

When you finish the course, the game sends the result to our server:

  • solo runs (alone on the raft, without bots): your total time, number of deaths and the date, under your nickname;
  • online games: the same, under the whole crew of players who finished together;
  • how many times you (or your crew) have finished, the game's version number and the type of device (for example Android).

We also keep how many times you have changed your nickname (the first change is free) and when the game last checked in with our server.

Multiplayer

When you host or join an online game, the game uses Unity Gaming Services: an anonymous sign-in that gives your device a random player ID, Unity Lobby to look up 4-digit join codes, and Unity Relay to connect the players. To route the game's traffic these services process your device's IP address. When you play over local Wi-Fi, your device's local network address is shared with the other devices in the game. In both cases your nickname is shared with the other players in the game.

Voice chat

In multiplayer games you can talk to the other players. This only happens if you allow the game to use the microphone and turn the mic on. Your voice is sent live, through Unity's Vivox service, to the players in your game only. We do not record or store voice chat. You can keep the mic off at any time, in the game's settings or with the mic button.

Advertising

The free game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), information about your device, and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another place where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Settings on your device

Your settings, such as sound and music volume, camera distance and the layout of the touch controls, are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Purchases

If the game offers a purchase (for example a nickname change after the free one, or removing ads), it is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; for a nickname change, our server is told only that the purchase was made, so it can apply the new name.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location, and we don't ask you to create an account or sign in.

2. How we use the information

  • to run the leaderboards and show your best times;
  • to keep each nickname and tag unique and stop others from using yours;
  • to keep track of your nickname changes;
  • to connect multiplayer games and voice chat;
  • to show ads, which keep the game free;
  • to keep the game working, fix problems and prevent cheating and abuse.

3. Who we share it with

We use the following service providers, which process data for us under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database of nicknames, tags and leaderboard results (servers in the European Union): supabase.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies, for anonymous sign-in, Lobby, Relay and Vivox voice chat: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google, for AdMob ads, the consent tool and Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your nickname, tag and results are also visible to everyone who plays the game, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

Your nickname, tag and results are kept for as long as the game's leaderboards are running, unless you ask us to delete them. Voice chat is not stored. The information saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game; if you reinstall, you get a new tag, and your old results stay on the leaderboard under the old one unless you ask us to remove them.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: email redgrapesgames01@gmail.com with your nickname and tag (shown in the game under Settings > Game, "YOU ARE NAME #TAG"). We will delete your nickname, tag and results within 30 days.
  • Change your nickname in Settings > Game > Change Name.
  • Voice chat: keep the mic off, or don't allow the microphone permission.
  • Ads: use your device's ad settings, or the consent choices offered by the game where they apply.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us at the address above.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Information sent between the game and our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Our database can't be read or changed directly from the internet: the game can only add results through checked functions, and the device code is stored only as a hash. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we work to protect yours.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the European Union and the United States. Where they do, they protect it as their privacy policies describe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
